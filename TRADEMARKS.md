# Names, marks and official services

The source code in this repository is (or will be, once a licence is adopted) available under an open-source licence. That licence covers **code**. It does not grant rights to the project's names and identity.

## Reserved to the project owner

- The name **NTO** and **NTO Studio**, **NTO Cloud**, **NTO Gallery**, **NTO Commerce**, **NTO Mobile** as product names.
- The **nto.motion** name and domain.
- The NTO logo and O motif as brand assets, when published.
- The description **"official"** for any build, service or website.

These are used by the project owner to identify the official project and its hosted services. Nothing in the code licence permits using them in a way that suggests a fork, build or service is the official NTO or is endorsed by it.

## What you can do

- Use, modify and redistribute the code under its licence.
- Say truthfully that your software is "based on NTO" or "a fork of NTO", provided the overall presentation does not imply it is the official project.
- Refer to NTO by name in documentation, reviews and comparisons.

## What needs permission

- Distributing a build under the NTO name or with the NTO logo.
- Operating a hosted service called NTO Cloud, NTO Gallery, or similar, or otherwise presenting a service as an official NTO service.
- Using nto.motion or confusingly similar names.

If you distribute a modified version, give it its own name and identity. The application's bundle identifier (`motion.nto.studio`) should also be changed in a redistributed build.

## Status

This is a statement of how the project intends to treat its identity. It is not a claim that any registration exists, and it is not legal advice. The owner may replace it with formal brand terms; until then, when in doubt, ask by opening an issue.
